# Go-to-market

## Launch: Pakistan, Bangladesh, Nigeria

Recruit through parent communities, counselors, schools, tutoring networks, university-access groups, and diaspora referrals. Lead with a free diagnostic and parent webinar; segment cohorts by timezone and price sensitivity.

## Secondary: Indonesia, Malaysia, South Korea

Validate messaging through partner-led webinars and small regional pilots before adding class inventory. Test whether affordability, English-language support, or accountability is the strongest entry point.

## Haiti sponsored access

Use NGO, school, diaspora, and scholarship partnerships. Measure sponsored seats, attendance, diagnostic completion, parent/guardian communication, and partner renewal intent.

## Parent webinar funnel

Ad/referral → webinar registration → reminder sequence → live teaching sample → diagnostic → founder offer → 48-hour follow-up → cohort placement.

## School and NGO funnel

Targeted outreach → needs call → sponsored or paid pilot proposal → diagnostic day → cohort launch → group participation report → renewal decision.
